Lasagna

Description

A classic baked pasta dish with layers of meat sauce, noodles, and cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Boil lasagna noodles according to package directions; drain.
  3. Brown ground beef in a skillet, drain fat, then stir in marinara sauce.
  4. In a bowl, mix ricotta, egg, Italian seasoning, and a pinch of salt/pepper.
  5. In a baking dish, layer: meat sauce, noodles, ricotta mix, mozzarella.
  6. Repeat layers and finish with mozzarella + parmesan on top.
  7. Cover with foil and bake 25 minutes.
  8. Remove foil and bake 15 more minutes until bubbly and golden.
  9. Rest 10 minutes before slicing.
