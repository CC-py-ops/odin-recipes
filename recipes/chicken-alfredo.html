Chicken Alfredo

Description

Creamy pasta with pan-seared chicken and a rich Alfredo sauce.

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 12 oz fettuccine
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Parsley (optional garnish)

Steps

  1. Cook fettuccine according to package instructions; drain and set aside.
  2. Season chicken with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat; cook chicken 5–6 minutes per side until done. Remove and slice.
  4. In the same pan, melt butter over medium heat. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds.
  5. Add heavy cream; simmer gently for 2–3 minutes.
  6. Stir in parmesan until smooth and creamy.
  7. Add cooked pasta and toss to coat.
  8. Top with sliced chicken and extra parmesan/parsley if desired.
